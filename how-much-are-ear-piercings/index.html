Price Breakdown

Ear Piercing Cost by Type

Prices include the service fee and starter implant-grade titanium jewelry at a professional studio. Prices vary by location and piercer experience — these are national averages for the United States in 2026.

Lobe

$20–$50

The most affordable ear piercing. Lobe piercings at reputable studios typically include implant-grade titanium or steel starter jewelry. Avoid piercing kiosks and gun piercings regardless of price — the savings aren't worth the increased healing complications.

Helix / Flat

$30–$60

Standard cartilage piercings sit in the $30–$60 range at most quality studios. The flat piercing may be slightly higher if it requires a more specialized flat-back labret that isn't in the standard inventory.

Tragus

$30–$65

Tragus piercings are moderately priced cartilage piercings. The small-diameter starter jewelry required for a tragus may cost slightly more than a standard helix flat-back, contributing to the slightly higher upper range.

Daith

$40–$80

Daith piercings require precise placement through the innermost cartilage fold and a specialized curved starter ring. The higher skill requirement and specialized jewelry push pricing into the $40–$80 range at most professional studios.

Conch

$40–$80

Both inner and outer conch piercings fall in the $40–$80 range. The large-diameter starter jewelry needed for a conch hoop typically costs more than a standard flat-back stud, which contributes to the higher end of this range.

Industrial

$40–$85

An industrial piercing requires two separate holes and a single long barbell. Many studios charge for each hole individually, making the total cost effectively two cartilage piercings. Expect to pay $40–$85 including the barbell.

WHAT AFFECTS PRICE

Why Prices Vary So Much

Studio Location

Studios in major metropolitan areas (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago) charge significantly more than studios in smaller cities or rural areas due to higher rent and cost of living. The same piercing from a comparable-quality piercer may cost $30 in a mid-sized city and $70 in a major market. This is normal and reflects operating costs, not quality differences.

Piercer Experience & Reputation

A senior APP-certified piercer with a social media following and a waitlist charges more than a new piercer at a walk-in studio. For complex placements like the daith, rook, or snug, this premium is absolutely worth paying — technique matters far more for inner-ear placements than for a standard lobe. Cheap piercings from inexperienced piercers routinely lead to costly corrections.

Jewelry Material

Starter jewelry at budget studios is often implant-grade steel or basic titanium, included in the service fee. Premium studios may include higher-quality titanium in the base price or offer upgrades. Choosing solid 14k gold starter jewelry can add $50–$200+ to the total cost depending on the design and the maker (BVLA, Anatometal, etc.). This is worth it for longevity and comfort.

Hidden Costs to Budget For

Don't forget aftercare products — sterile saline spray costs $10–$20 and you'll need it daily for months. A travel pillow for cartilage piercings is another $15–$30. Jewelry upgrades at the 6–12 month mark are typically $40–$150+ depending on metal and design. If you are building a multiple piercing curated ear, total budget across all piercings and upgrades can reach $300–$800 over 1–2 years.

Is a Cheap Ear Piercing Worth It?

In short: no. A $10 gun piercing at a mall kiosk will use blunt jewelry, create more tissue trauma than a needle, and use mystery-alloy jewelry that is a common cause of allergic reactions. The resulting healing complications — extended soreness, keloid formation, rejection — will cost far more in time and money to resolve than the initial savings. For healing time and safety information, see our dedicated guides.

A $35–$50 lobe piercing at an APP-affiliated studio with implant-grade titanium jewelry, a clean technique, and proper aftercare instructions is an investment that pays for itself in a smooth, complication-free healing experience. Review our full piercing chart for complete cost data by type.
